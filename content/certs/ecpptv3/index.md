eCPPTv3 is INE's practical penetration-testing certification. It tests the full chain, from enumeration to a foothold to a fully compromised network, on real machines.

## What I learned

- **A repeatable methodology**: scoping, enumeration, exploitation, post-exploitation, and documenting what I did.
- **Exploiting hosts and services** with Nmap, Metasploit and manual techniques when the automated route fails.
- **Privilege escalation** on Windows and Linux. Windows privilege escalation was the hardest part for me and taught me the most.
- **Pivoting** through compromised hosts to reach internal networks that are not directly visible.
- **Active Directory fundamentals** on the offensive side, which later made CRTP much easier to approach.

## Why it matters to my work

It trained me to think in attack chains instead of single findings. When I investigate an alert in the SOC, I now ask what the attacker would try next and where that step would appear in the logs.

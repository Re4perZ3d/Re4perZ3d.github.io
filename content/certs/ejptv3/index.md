eJPTv3 was my first practical offensive-security certification. There are no multiple-choice questions: you attack a lab network and answer based on what you find.

## What I learned

- **Networking and host discovery**: mapping a network and finding what is alive and what is exposed.
- **Enumeration** of services with Nmap and related tools, and why careful enumeration decides how fast you move later.
- **Vulnerability assessment and exploitation basics** using Metasploit, plus the manual checks behind them.
- **Web and password attack fundamentals**, and how to document findings clearly.

## Why it matters to my work

It built the foundation for everything that came after, eCPPTv3 and CRTP included, and gave me the habit of enumerating thoroughly before touching anything.

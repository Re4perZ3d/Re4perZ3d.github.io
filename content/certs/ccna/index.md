The three CCNA courses (1, 2 and 3) were my first step into networking. Every later skill in security rests on knowing how a network actually works.

## What I learned

- **CCNA 1, Introduction to Networks**: how data moves, the OSI and TCP/IP models, IPv4 and IPv6 addressing and subnetting.
- **CCNA 2, Switching, Routing and Wireless Essentials**: VLANs, spanning tree, inter-VLAN routing, static and dynamic routing, DHCP and wireless basics.
- **CCNA 3, Enterprise Networking, Security and Automation**: OSPF, NAT, access control lists, WAN concepts, network security and an introduction to automation.

## Why it matters to my work

Reading a PCAP, understanding why a connection looks suspicious, or following lateral movement is much easier when the networking underneath is solid. This is where that foundation started.
